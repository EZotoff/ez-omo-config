---
description: Specialist design review — draft, then critique, then challenge
---

Run a three-stage specialist design review on the design/proposal at hand. The composition is visible and editable — before spawning, restate the plan below and let the operator adjust stages, ordering, or models if they want; proceed as-adjusted.

Stage 1 — DRAFT: one `artistry` (or best available creative-generalist) agent produces the design/proposal draft from the brief. Give it full context and freedom on approach.

Stage 2 — CRITIQUE: one `oracle` agent reviews the draft for soundness: flaws, missing considerations, failure modes. It sees the draft but not stage 1's conversation.

Stage 3 — CHALLENGE: one agent in the `mephistopheles` category attacks the critiqued draft — adversarial pass on the strongest assumptions. It sees the draft and the critique.

Then synthesize: the final design incorporating what survived, with each material change attributed to which stage forced it, and the open disagreements stated plainly rather than smoothed over.

Boundary: this is analysis, not authorization — the output informs the operator's decision; nothing proceeds anywhere without them saying so.
